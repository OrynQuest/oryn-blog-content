> - Look early, keep a backup plan, and leave room for rest.

Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26 this year. For many Orange County families, that means a school break that arrives just as everyone is tired from a busy fall term. A little planning now can turn the week from a scramble into something the whole family enjoys.

Here is a simple way to plan it.

## First, check how long your break actually is

Orange County has many school districts, plus charter and private schools, and their calendars do not all match. Some give the full week of Thanksgiving off. Others start the break partway through the week. If your children attend different schools, they may not even be off on the same days.

Before you plan anything, write down three things:

1. **Which days each child is off school.**
2. **Which days you and any other caregivers are working.**
3. **Which days are already spoken for**, such as travel, hosting or visiting family.

The gaps between those lists are the days you are actually planning for. Often it is fewer days than it first seems.

## Plan the shape of the week before the activities

It is tempting to start with a list of fun things. Starting with the shape of the week works better.

- **Anchor the fixed days first.** Thanksgiving Day, travel days and any day you are cooking or hosting.
- **Put structured activities on work days.** A camp day or a drop-in class is most useful when a parent needs to be somewhere else.
- **Keep some days open on purpose.** Kids come into the break tired too. Unscheduled time, a slow morning or a lazy afternoon is part of the point of a break.
- **Mix new and familiar.** One new activity to try, plus something your child already loves, keeps the week interesting without making it stressful.

## Kinds of activities to look for

Not every class or camp runs during school holidays, and schedules can change around Thanksgiving. Treat any plan as something to check, not something to assume. Activities worth looking for include:

- **Short camps.** Ask about daily hours, what to pack, and whether lunch is included.
- **Drop-in classes.** A single session of art, cooking, sports or STEM can fill one day without a longer commitment.
- **Online classes.** These can be handy on a travel day, a rainy day, or when a younger sibling is napping.
- **A class your child already takes.** If it meets during the break, it can be a familiar anchor in an unusual week. If it does not, the break is a good time to try something different.

When you find something that looks right, message the vendor with practical questions: drop-off and pickup times, what to bring, and how the day is structured.

## Orange County things to plan around

A few local details are worth building into your plan:

- **Weather.** Late November in Orange County is often mild and sunny, but it can also bring early-season rain or dry Santa Ana winds. Keep an indoor backup for any outdoor plan.
- **Traffic.** The days around Thanksgiving are some of the busiest of the year on the roads. On the day before Thanksgiving especially, activities close to home are easier on everyone.
- **Short days.** In late November it gets dark before 5 p.m., so outdoor plans work better earlier in the day.
- **Busy weekends.** The weekend after Thanksgiving is a popular time for shopping and outings, so shared spaces may be more crowded than usual.

## Free and low-key ideas between bookings

Not every day needs a booking. Some easy ideas for the open days:

- **A fall leaf hunt.** Some Orange County neighborhoods and parks have trees that turn color this time of year. Collect leaves and press them in a heavy book.
- **A long walk somewhere new.** A park trail, a harbor walk or a stretch of beach path, early in the day while it is light and cool.
- **A library visit.** Pick a stack of books for the week, including a few about the holiday.
- **Kitchen helpers.** Let kids measure, stir and decorate. Even small jobs make them part of the holiday.
- **A gratitude project.** Make cards for relatives, or keep a jar where everyone adds one thing they are thankful for each day.
- **A blanket fort afternoon.** Simple, free, and usually a hit.

## FAQ

### When is Thanksgiving break for Orange County schools in 2026?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026. How many days children have off around it depends on the school district or school, and some give the whole week while others give part of it. Check your school's published calendar for the exact dates.

### How far ahead should I look for Thanksgiving break activities?

As soon as you know your break dates. Sessions during school breaks can fill up, especially on work days when many families need care. If a session you want is full on ORYN Quest, join the waitlist; when a seat opens, the next family gets a 24-hour hold.

### What if my kids have different break days?

Plan around the child with the shortest break first, since those are the days you need care for only some of your children. Then look for drop-in sessions or short activities that cover the extra days for the child who is off longer.

### What can we do if it rains during the break?

Keep a short indoor list ready: an online class, a library visit, baking together, a craft project or a blanket fort. On ORYN Quest, Explore lets you choose in person or online, so you can check for online sessions on your open days.

### Can I find a camp for just one or two days?

Explore lists camps and drop-ins. A drop-in is a single session, so it can cover one day. For a camp, open the listing to see how its sessions are set up and which dates have open seats, and message the vendor if anything is unclear.
