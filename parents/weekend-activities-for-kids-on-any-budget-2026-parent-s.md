## Free and Low-Cost Weekend Ideas That Still Feel Special

Some of the best family weekend ideas in Southern California cost little more than gas, sunscreen, and a full water bottle. For families searching for affordable kids activities near me, these are reliable places to start.

* California Science Center in Exposition Park, Los Angeles, offers free general admission. Paid extras such as IMAX films can be added if you want them. Arrive near opening on busy weekends.
* Griffith Observatory offers free admission and free public telescope viewing on clear evenings. Parking fills early, so plan to beat the crowds.
* Cabrillo Marine Aquarium in San Pedro is free with a suggested donation. Its smaller rooms and gentle pace suit young children well.
* Public library story times and STEM events are free, weekly, and air conditioned during hot months. They are a dependable option for toddlers and early readers.
* Tide pools at Abalone Cove in Rancho Palos Verdes or Crystal Cove State Park are worth the drive. Parking fees apply, then the exploring is free. Check a tide chart and go at low tide to see anemones, hermit crabs, and small fish.
* Eaton Canyon in Pasadena has free entry, a shady nature center, and a popular waterfall hike. Weekend parking fills before midmorning.
