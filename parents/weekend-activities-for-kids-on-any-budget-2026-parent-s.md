## Free and Low-Cost Weekend Ideas That Still Feel Special

Some of the best family weekend ideas in Southern California cost little more than gas, parking, and a full water bottle. For families searching for affordable kids activities near me, these are reliable places to start.

* California Science Center in Exposition Park, Los Angeles, offers free general admission. Paid extras such as IMAX films can be added if you want them, and parking fees apply. Arrive near opening on busy weekends.
* Griffith Observatory offers free admission and free public telescope viewing on clear evenings. Parking at the Observatory is paid and fills early, so plan to beat the crowds, or take the DASH Observatory bus up from the Vermont/Sunset Metro station.
* Cabrillo Marine Aquarium in San Pedro is free with a suggested donation, and parking fees apply at Cabrillo Beach. Its smaller rooms and gentle pace suit young children well.
* Public library story times and STEM events are free, many branches hold them every week, and the rooms are air conditioned during hot months. They are a dependable option for toddlers and early readers.
* Tide pools at Crystal Cove State Park in Orange County are worth the drive. Parking fees apply, then the exploring is free. Check a tide chart and go at low tide to see anemones, hermit crabs, and small fish.
