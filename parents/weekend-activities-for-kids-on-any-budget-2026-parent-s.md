## Free and Low-Cost Weekend Ideas for Southern California Families
![Free and Low-Cost Weekend Ideas for Southern California Families - some, best, family](/blog/weekend-activities-for-kids-on-any-budget-2026-parent-s-s2.png)


Some of the best family weekend ideas in Southern California cost nothing but gas and sunscreen:

- **California Science Center** (Exposition Park, Los Angeles): free general admission, with paid extras like IMAX films if you want them. Arrive near opening on weekends.
- **Griffiffth Observatory**: free admission all day, plus free public telescope viewing on clear evenings. Parking fills by mid-morning, so go early or take the shuttle.
- **Cabrillo Marine Aquarium** (San Pedro): free with a suggested donation, and a gentle, low-stimulation layout that works well for toddlers.
- **Public library story times and STEM events**: free, weekly, and air-conditioned, which matters from June through October. Many SoCal library systems, including Los Angeles Public Library, also let cardholders check out museum passes through programs like Discover & Go.
- **Tide pools at Abalone Cove** (Rancho Palos Verdes) or **Crystal Cove State Park** (small parking fee): check a tide chart and go at low tide, when anemones and hermit crabs put on a quiet show.
- **Eaton Canyon** (Pasadena): free entry, a shady nature center, and a popular waterfall hike. Weekend parking fills early, so arrive before 9 a.m.
