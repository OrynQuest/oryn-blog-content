> **Key takeaways**
> - Start wide with a category or a search, then narrow. Set your child's age next.
> - Activity Type and Format sort out classes, camps, drop-ins and birthday parties, in person or virtual.
> - The needs filters are there if they matter to your family. Skip them if they don't.
> - There is no day, time or city filter. Sort by Distance or open Map View, then read a listing's sessions.
> - Every listing has its own credit price. Check it before you book, and message the vendor with questions.

Type "find kids activities near me" into a search engine and you get a long page of links, with little about whether any of them suits your child. On ORYN Quest, the Explore page can do that sorting in about five minutes. Here is the order we would use, one minute at a time.

An honest note first. ORYN Quest is a marketplace: independent local businesses (we call them vendors) list classes, camps, drop-ins and birthday parties, and families book them. ORYN does not run the activities. What you see depends on what vendors near you have listed, and in some areas that list is still short.

## Minute one: choose a way in

Open [Explore](/explore) on orynquest.com. You have two ways in.

**Pick a category** if you know what your child likes. Categories include Arts & Crafts, Sports & Fitness, STEM & Science, Music & Dance, Tutoring & Education, Therapy & Wellness, Camps & Outdoors, Cooking & Baking, and Language & Reading.

**Type a search** if you have something specific in mind. Try plain words: "pottery", "chess", "soccer".

Not sure yet? Skip both and go straight to age. Browsing everything for your child's age can turn up something you hadn't thought of.

## Minute two: set your child's age

Set the Child's Age filter next. The choices are Any Age, Infants (0-2), Toddlers (3-5), Kids (6-9), Tweens (10-12) and Teens (13+).

Pick the band your child is in today. Some listings also show their own age range on the listing page, which can be narrower than the band, so check it when you open one.

Two children in different bands? Run the search once for each.

## Minute three: type, format and level

Three more menus tidy the list, and a fourth watches cost.

| Filter | Choices | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| Activity Type | Classes, Camps, Drop-in, Birthday Parties | You know the shape you want: a regular class, a camp, a one-off visit or a party |
| Format | In-Person, Virtual | You want a room to go to, or something at home on a screen |
| Difficulty | Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, All Levels | Your child is brand new to it, or already has some skill |
| Max Credit Cost | Any Price, or one of a few credit limits | You want to see only listings up to a certain number of credits |

For a first try at something new, Drop-in, Beginner or All Levels make a gentle start. Leave Max Credit Cost on Any Price at first if you just want to see what is out there.

## Minute four: needs filters, then sort

If your child has particular needs, four switches can help:

- **Inclusive** or **Autism-friendly**. This is one filter with two labels: Inclusive in the website's desktop filter panel, Autism-friendly on phones and tablets and in the app.
- **Sensory-friendly**
- **Wheelchair Accessible**
- **Insurance Accepted**

If none apply, leave them off; each one makes the list shorter. A filter narrows the list but doesn't replace a conversation, so if a need matters a lot, message the vendor before you book.

Now sort. The sort menu offers Recommended, Distance, Price (low to high or high to low), Highest Rated and Newest. There is no filter for day of the week, time of day or city, so **Distance** is your "near me" button: it puts the closest listings first.

Prefer a picture? **Map View** shows listings on a map, handy when "near" means near school rather than home.

If the list comes back empty, take off the last filter you added, or use **Clear All** and start again with just age.

## Minute five: open a listing and read it properly

Open the one that looks right. A listing shows its title, category, description, photos, credit price per seat, length, location, upcoming sessions with open seats, and reviews. Some listings also show an age range, a skill level, accessibility accommodations or a short video.

Read it in this order:

1. **Available Slots.** These are the sessions, and this is where days and times live. If none fit your week, try the next listing.
2. **Location and length.** Think about the drive and what comes after.
3. **Description and photos.** Does the space look like somewhere your child would happily walk into?
4. **Reviews.** Families who have been can tell you how a first visit felt.
5. **Credit price.** Every class has its own credit price. Check it before you book: there is no card step, and credits come off your balance.

Found two good options? Sign in and tap the heart to save them. The **Saved** button beside the search box takes you back to your saved activities. For help weighing them, see [How to compare children's activity classes before you book](/blog/how-to-compare-children-s-activity-classes-before-you-book).

## Still have a question? Message the vendor

A listing won't answer everything. Sign in, tap **Message Vendor** and ask before you book. A few questions worth sending:

- What does a first session look like for someone new?
- Can a parent stay and watch?
- My child finds loud rooms hard. How do you usually handle that?

For what a good first visit should feel like, see [What a great first class actually looks like](/blog/what-a-great-first-class-actually-looks-like-and-what-to-ask).

## The shortcut: ask Lanti

If you'd rather not tap through filters, Lanti, our assistant, can do the searching. Lanti is built into the website and the family app, and you can type or talk.

Lanti searches by category and place, not by day or time. A request like "find a cooking class for my 6-year-old in Los Angeles" is the right shape. Lanti can also filter by needs such as autism-friendly or sensory-friendly, show listings near you and find similar listings.

You still pick the session from the listing. If Lanti prepares a booking, nothing happens until you tap **Confirm** on Lanti's card. Lanti never books on its own.

## FAQ

### Can I search for activities by day or time?

Not with a filter. There is no filter for day of the week, time of day or city. Sort by Distance, then open a listing to see its upcoming sessions.

### Does ORYN Quest have online classes?

The Format filter has In-Person and Virtual. Whether virtual listings show up depends on what vendors have listed, so set Format to Virtual and look.

### How do I find activities for a child with extra needs?

Use the needs filters: Inclusive or Autism-friendly (one filter, labelled differently depending on the screen), Sensory-friendly, Wheelchair Accessible and Insurance Accepted. Then message the vendor before you book.

### Can Lanti book an activity for me?

Lanti can find listings and prepare a booking, but nothing happens until you tap Confirm on Lanti's card.

### Why do I only see a few results near me?

Results depend on what vendors near you have listed. Try Any Age, fewer filters or Map View. ORYN Quest serves Southern California today; outside it, join the waiting list at orynquest.com/waitlist.
