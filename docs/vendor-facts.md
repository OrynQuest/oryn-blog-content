## 0. Basics

- ORYN Quest is a marketplace. Families use it to book kids' activities that independent local businesses run. ORYN does not run the activities and never takes custody of children.
- Families book with credits from monthly membership plans. The credit price shown at checkout is the price charged. Families pay ORYN by card through Stripe, and ORYN collects that money as the vendor's limited agent.
- Families find and book classes on orynquest.com and in the ORYN Quest family app on the App Store and Google Play.
- Vendors are independent businesses, not ORYN employees, agents or franchisees. They handle their own staff, permits and taxes.
- ORYN Quest, Inc. is a Delaware corporation based in Glendale, CA. Support: support@orynquest.com.

## 1. Who can join and how

- Businesses, nonprofits, schools, instructors, tutors, coaches, camps, museums and sports groups offering kids' activities can apply. The person applying must be 18+ and allowed to sign for the business.
- How to apply: create an account at orynquest.com/vendor-apply, fill in the business profile, and press Submit. You can save a draft and finish later. The public pitch page is orynquest.com/vendors.
- The form asks for business name and category (required), plus description, address, ZIP, access/parking info, amenities, phone, website, tags, and whether you accept insurance. An AI helper can draft the description.
- To submit, you tick two boxes: you agree to the Vendor Agreement and Code of Conduct, and you confirm your business carries general liability insurance. The server refuses an application without the insurance box.
- That insurance box is self-reported. ORYN sets no minimum amount, asks for no certificate and does not check it. ORYN does not insure vendors.
- Service area: today ORYN is open **only in Southern California**, meaning business ZIP codes starting 900 through 935. Outside it, you can't apply and are sent to the vendor waiting list.
- The vendor waiting list asks only for name, business and area. It needs no application and no bank details. ORYN emails you when your area opens.
- After you submit, your status is "pending". You get a notice and a confirmation email. An ORYN admin then approves or declines. Only an admin can approve.
- ORYN reviews the business information you send. Approval is **not** a license check, inspection, insurance audit, background check or endorsement.
- ORYN does not check licenses, certifications, insurance, staff qualifications or accessibility claims itself. Vendors vouch for these.
- ORYN does not run or require criminal background checks. Each vendor screens its own staff as the law requires.
- If approved, you get an email and can publish listings. If declined, you get an email with the reason (if one was given) and can fix and resubmit.
- Joining costs $0 today: no setup fee, no subscription, and nothing taken from the price you set.
- One vendor business per account. The owner can also book as a parent, but not their own classes.
- Vendors agree to tell ORYN about a serious safety incident within 24 hours.

## 2. Listings

- Only approved vendors can list. A listing can be a class, camp, birthday party or drop-in, held in person, online, or both.
- To go live, a listing needs: title, category, dollar price per seat, length, location (full street address if in person), at least one photo, and a waiver answer. Drafts can skip the photo and waiver answer, and families never see drafts.
- Optional details: description, age range (the form takes 0–18), skill level, total seats, accessibility accommodations, access info, one short video (up to 100 MB), a waitlist, a last-minute deal, "accepts insurance", and camp or party extras.
- "ORYN Seats" lets a vendor limit how many seats in a class can be booked through ORYN.
- Photos: at least 1 to publish, up to 5 in the form. An optional AI "photo coach" gives tips but never blocks publishing.
- Photos must be yours or licensed to you. You keep ownership, and ORYN may use them to run and promote the platform. ORYN may review, edit for formatting, or remove any listing.
- Waiver question: before a listing goes live, the vendor must choose one of three answers. Families sign **in the app before booking**, sign **on paper at the first session**, or there is **no waiver**. Even ORYN admins cannot publish a class without this answer.
- "In the app" needs at least one required waiver switched on, and a family can't finish booking until they sign. "On paper": the vendor collects and keeps it, and ORYN does not check it. With every answer, parents still accept ORYN's own Parent Waiver & Release before booking.
- ORYN does not review what a vendor's waiver says. An AI tool can turn a photo or PDF of a paper waiver into editable text. You check it before saving, and the upload is then deleted.
- Schedule: add time slots one at a time, or a whole term at once from a repeating pattern (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly). Listings can be paused or archived.
- **Recurring enrollment** (a family enrolls for a term and is charged automatically) is built but **switched OFF** for the public.

## 3. Pricing and money

- The vendor sets its own price, in **dollars per booked seat**. The form calls it "the dollar amount you receive per booked seat".
- ORYN turns that price into credits for families, **rounding up**. The vendor is paid exactly its dollar price, and ORYN keeps the small rounding extra. Example at today's rate: a $20 class shows families 23 credits, and the vendor gets $20.00.
- The vendor can't set a separate credit price for a dollar-priced class. Families see only the credits, never the vendor's dollar figure.
- Today's default payout rate is 88 cents per credit. ORYN earns from the gap between what families pay for plans and what vendors are paid, about 28% on average at today's settings. That gap is **not** taken out of the vendor's price. ORYN can change these settings going forward, with notice.
- The vendor is paid the price the listing had **when the family booked**, even if it changes later.
- Discounts ORYN pays for don't cut vendor pay: promo codes, last-minute deals, and the 10% sibling discount.
- Last-minute deal: a vendor can offer 5–75% off sessions starting within 24 hours that still have seats. ORYN pays the discount.
- Vendors are paid only for **completed** sessions (checked out, or completed by the platform or ORYN).
- **How vendors are paid today:** ORYN's finance team sends a bank transfer. Stripe payouts to vendors are switched OFF.
- **When:** there is no fixed payout day. ORYN must start each payout within 30 days after the end of the month the session was completed in, once your bank details are confirmed.
- Bank details: the vendor enters only the last 4 digits of its account and routing numbers. ORYN confirms the full numbers directly before the first transfer. Each payout is recorded at the full amount earned, with no fee taken out, and shows on the Payouts page with its status and reference code.
- Stripe payouts (monthly, $50 minimum, 14-day wait, optional paid "Get paid now") are in the Vendor Agreement and built, but **switched OFF** today. "Get paid now" is hidden and refused while off. ORYN may switch Stripe on after telling vendors.
- ORYN may hold a payout while it looks into fraud or a dispute, and may take refunds and chargebacks out of future payouts. Payouts are in US dollars.
- Vendors handle their own taxes. ORYN keeps earnings records and issues tax forms (such as 1099s) where the law requires.

## 4. Bookings, cancellations, no-shows

- Vendors get an email for every new booking. Families get a reminder about one day before class.
- Family cancellations use one schedule for every listing: more than 48 hours before, 100% of credits back; 24–48 hours, 50%; under 24 hours, nothing. Families can move a booking to another time for free until class starts or the child is checked in.
- If the vendor cancels, the family automatically gets 100% back, however close to class time. A vendor can't cancel after the child is checked in or class has started. Frequent cancelling can hurt a vendor's ranking.
- Check-in opens **1 hour before** class. Check-out, "complete" and "no-show" open **when class starts**.
- Once a child is checked in, the family can't cancel or move that booking in the app. They must contact support.
- A vendor can undo a mistaken check-in before check-out, on the day of the class (or from the evening before, for classes starting before 1 a.m.).
- A checked-in child can't be marked a no-show. A vendor can undo a no-show unless the session was cancelled.
- Checking a child out completes the session, which makes it payable. If a vendor checks a child in but forgets to check out, the platform completes the session on its own within a few hours of class ending. A session with no check-in is **not** auto-completed; a person reviews it.
- Waitlist: the vendor turns it on per listing and sets a maximum size (default 30). When a seat opens, the next family gets a 24-hour hold. Unused holds pass to the next family (checked every 15 minutes).
- On the waitlist, a vendor sees the parent's name, the child's first name, and each family's place in line. It gets no contact details.

## 5. Talking to families (and privacy)

- ORYN has built-in messaging. A vendor can start a chat only with families who booked with it, and families can message any listed vendor. Vendors get an email when a parent's message sits unread for 30+ minutes.
- Messages are checked automatically. Slurs and threats are blocked. Messages with dollar prices, Venmo/Zelle-style requests, or contact details meant to move a booking off ORYN are flagged for review, and the sender sees a notice.
- The rule: no quoting dollar prices in chat, and no asking for payment outside ORYN for bookings made on ORYN. Vendors can still run their business outside ORYN. Messaging is adults only, never with children.
- For each booking a vendor sees the parent's name and picture, and the child's name, picture, age, and autism-friendly and sensory-support flags. Vendors do **not** get the parent's email or phone through bookings, or the family's insurance or medical notes. (In-app waiver records do include the signer's name and email.)
- Vendors may use family information only to run the booked activity. They must never sell it or build marketing lists from it.
- A vendor's public profile shows its name, city, address, amenities, rating, logo, social links, and phone and website if added.

## 6. Tools vendors get

- The vendor web portal has: Home (dashboard), Bookings, Attendance, Parent feedback, Schedule, Messages, Listings, Waivers, Revenue Report, Payouts and Profile. It also works in a phone browser.
- The dashboard and Revenue Report show earnings (this month, last month, all time, by month and by listing, completed sessions only), a 6-month chart and quick insights.
- Category benchmarks show averages for your category only when at least 5 other vendors are in it. Other vendors' numbers are never shown. Listing view counts cover signed-in visitors only.
- Reviews: only a parent with a completed booking can review (1–5 stars, one per booking). Families are asked 1–72 hours after class, and vendors can reply publicly.
- Quality rule: with 3+ reviews, an average below 4.0 stars puts a vendor on probation automatically. Listings stay visible, and the vendor is restored at 4.0+. This is checked daily.
- After a completed class, a vendor can send the family short feedback: ratings for engagement, focus, social skills and confidence, strength tags, and a note.
- The portal has an AI assistant you can type or talk to. It can show listings, bookings, reviews, revenue and today's roster. It can draft listings, add time slots, message customers, reply to reviews, mark attendance, and suggest schedules.
- The assistant must get the vendor's tap on Confirm before it changes anything. The vendor stays responsible for what it helps write.
- ORYN Town: approved vendors appear as a free storefront on Main Street in ORYN's kids' game world. It shows only name, category and city: no prices, contacts or ads. Vendors can opt out any time, and placement is never sold.
- A vendor can close its own shop from Profile once no upcoming sessions remain, and is still paid what it's owed.
- A vendor phone app ("ORYN Quest Business") is **not** in the app stores yet. Vendors use the web portal.

## 7. Growth programmes

- Refer-a-business: a **parent** shares a link (orynquest.com/vendor-apply?ref=CODE). When that business completes its first booking with a real, unrelated family, the parent earns **10 credits today**, for up to 5 businesses in any rolling 12 months.
- The referred business itself gets no referral reward.
- Anyone sharing a referral link must say they earn credits for it.
- ORYN may feature vendor listings in its marketing (email, social media, ads) and invite vendors to optional promotions.
- Search ranking can depend on parent satisfaction, reply speed, completion and cancellation rates, and profile completeness. ORYN doesn't publish the weights.
- Parents find classes by search, on a map, and through ORYN's AI assistant. ORYN does not guarantee bookings, revenue, ranking or AI recommendations.

## 8. NEVER CLAIM (false or unproven today)

- ❌ Instant, same-day or on-demand payouts, a fixed payout day (for example "the 3rd"), or "within X days". The only promise is the 30-day outer limit.
- ❌ "Paid through Stripe", the $50 minimum, the 14-day wait, or "Get paid now". These are Stripe-only rules, and Stripe payouts are off.
- ❌ "No commission / no fees, **ever**". Once Stripe payouts are on, "Get paid now" carries a fee.
- ❌ Coverage outside Southern California (ZIPs 900–935). San Francisco and New York are NOT served.
- ❌ That the **ORYN Quest Business** app is in the App Store or on Google Play.
- ❌ Recurring enrollment or automatic term billing for families. It is switched off.
- ❌ "ORYN-verified", "vetted", "background-checked", "licensed", "insured" or "safety-certified" vendors, or any badge. ORYN also doesn't insure anyone or check insurance.
- ❌ Guaranteed bookings, income, full classes, top ranking or AI recommendations.
- ❌ That vendors set their own cancellation or refund rules, charge late fees, or get paid for no-shows or late cancellations.
- ❌ "Every vendor gets a storefront in ORYN Town", or that ORYN Town placement can be bought.
- ❌ That vendors can take cash or Venmo for ORYN bookings, or quote dollar prices in chat.
- ❌ That vendors get parents' phone numbers or emails with bookings.
- ❌ A referral bonus for vendors. Only parents earn the business-referral reward.
- ❌ A guaranteed approval time (for example "approved in 1–2 days").
- ❌ That ORYN runs, supervises or staffs activities, employs instructors, or endorses any named vendor. Vendors may not suggest endorsement either.
- ❌ Any ORYN number that is not on this sheet (per-plan margins, costs, counts).
- ❌ How long ORYN may use a vendor's photos after it leaves.

## UNVERIFIED — do not use

- Any approval time (for example "1–2 business days").
- partnerships@orynquest.com — use support@orynquest.com instead.
- That a new area opens by itself once enough people join its waiting list.
- Any 1099 dollar threshold.
- Paid "Featured · Sponsored" spots on Explore. Admins manage them; there is no public price or vendor sign-up.
- Counts (vendors, listings, families, bookings), plan prices, and when the Business app launches.
