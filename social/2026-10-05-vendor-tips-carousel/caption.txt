Thinking about listing your kids' classes on ORYN Quest? Here are 4 small things that help your vendor application go smoothly:

1. Sign up with your business email (the one at your website's address)
2. Add your business website
3. Type your address the way it appears on maps
4. Tick the agreement and insurance boxes

We check every application. If everything checks out, your first application may be approved automatically right after you submit. Otherwise a person on our team reviews it and we email you the decision.

Free to join. Open to kids' activity businesses in Southern California.
